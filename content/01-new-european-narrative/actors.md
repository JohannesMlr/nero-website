# New European Narrative and Consciousness — actors

People, organisations and initiatives doing notable work in this field.

## Johannes Müller — Renaissance Thoughts

NERO's own publication channel (Substack, bilingual German/English). The filed essay develops the
diagnosis side of this field — collective aimlessness, the defensive register of political communication,
and goal-avoidance as a symptom of an unanswered identity question. A companion essay on collective
identity, local and European, is announced but unwritten.

**Why it matters for this field:** this is where NERO's own narrative position is being worked out in
public. Foundation text, not independent evidence — build on it and quote it, never cite it as external
corroboration.

Sources: `../03-new-state-and-governance/sources/2025-11-23-goals-as-political-leadership-instrument.md`

## Aladin El-Mafaalani

Sociologist, author of *Misstrauensgemeinschaften*. Societies with a positive outlook on the future can
endure hardship and long periods of strain; spirals of mutual distrust and radicalisation begin only once
belief in a better tomorrow erodes.

**Why it matters for this field:** the strongest available argument that an aspirational story is
infrastructure rather than decoration — it is what buys a society tolerance for the reforms the other
action fields demand. Also the cleanest external support for a claim NERO otherwise makes on its own
authority.
