# New Technology Leadership — actors

People, organisations and initiatives doing notable work in this field.

## Rhine Group

Cross-sector reform forum launched August 2026, co-chaired by **Mario Draghi** and **Patrick Collison** (Stripe), with **Luis Garicano** as executive director. Sixty-plus members spanning economics (Aghion, Holmström, Gourinchas, Reichlin, Giavazzi, Weder di Mauro), technology and industry (Lütke/Shopify, Zennström/Atomico, Siemiatkowski/Klarna, Ferrari/Bending Spoons, Niel/Iliad, Pignataro/ION, Colao), and politics (Ilves, Le Maire). Not an EU institution; explicitly does not claim to substitute for the Commission or national governments.

**Why it matters for this field:** the most senior attempt yet to close the gap between the Draghi diagnosis and enacted reform, arguing technology leadership as a condition of European sovereignty rather than as industrial policy. Founder-heavy composition makes it structurally different from previous European reform bodies. Worth tracking as a potential ally and as a test case for whether convening power alone can unblock the reform agenda.

**Open exposure:** Swiss-law registration with offices at a Geneva trust company, own website on Webflow/AWS-Northern-Virginia, no Polish members and one representative from any post-2004 accession state (of 55 counted by Brussels Signal; the group claims 80+). Treat as a potential ally whose legitimacy is contestable — useful to engage, risky to cite uncritically.

## European Policy Innovation Council — Draghi Observatory

Tracks implementation of the Draghi report recommendations. As of July 2026: 60 of 383 recommendations fully implemented (15.7%), +0.6pp over six months.

**Why it matters for this field:** the only running scoreboard on whether Europe's competitiveness agenda is actually being executed. Useful as an independent metric for any claim about reform progress — including claims made by the Rhine Group.
