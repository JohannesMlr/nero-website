Johannes Mueller is the founder of NERO, the New European Renaissance Organisation.

He is also the founder of Workpath, a strategy execution and business management platform used by large enterprises across Europe. For close to a decade he has worked with organisations of hundreds of thousands of people, helping them build their operating models around outcomes and goals rather than processes and hierarchies.

He writes on European identity and sovereignty, and on what political institutions can learn from how organisations set, communicate and pursue goals. Based in Munich.
