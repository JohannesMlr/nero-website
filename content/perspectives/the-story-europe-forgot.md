title: The Story Europe Forgot to Tell Itself
teaser: Europe still inspires imitation across the world. It has simply stopped telling that story to its own citizens.
lede: Ask people abroad what Europe represents and you'll hear ambition. Ask Europeans the same question, and the answer is often smaller than the reality deserves.
pull: How can we promote European unity, dynamism and way of life through a strong and aspirational story of our continent?
---

Europe's civilisational record is, by any honest measure, extraordinary — in science, in culture, in the sheer density of ideas that reshaped how the world lives and governs itself. And yet the internal story Europeans tell about their own continent has drifted toward caution, apology and managed decline.

That gap between reality and narrative matters more than it sounds. Confidence is not just a mood; it is a precondition for the risk-taking that built the technology, capital markets and institutions this continent now needs to rebuild. A story of decline produces citizens who manage. A story of renaissance produces citizens who build.

None of this requires inventing anything. It requires the discipline to tell the true story — ambitious, plural, unfinished — with the same confidence competitors tell their own.
