pull: How can we make public good policy work again to enable excellent life quality, social mobility and trust in our model of society?
---

Much of the infrastructure Europeans rely on daily was designed for a different century, financed by a different generation, and is now reaching the point where patching it costs more than reimagining it. That is an unusually rare opportunity: a forced rebuild that can be shaped by today's priorities instead of yesterday's constraints.

The old model treated public infrastructure as something the state built once and maintained forever. The better model treats it as a living system — instrumented, adaptive, and funded in a way that makes long-term maintenance as politically rewarding as the ribbon-cutting was.

Getting this right over the next decade will do more for European quality of life than almost any other single policy area, precisely because it is the invisible layer everything else depends on.
