## Cross-referenced finds

- **Rhine Group** (Aug 2026) — Draghi/Collison reform forum. Relevant here as a governance experiment: a body with no formal powers betting that convening power plus commissioned evidence can unblock a stalled reform agenda. Baseline to judge it against: 60 of 383 Draghi recommendations fully implemented by July 2026 (15.7%). It also raises the governance question in its purest form: who authorises reform when the elected machinery cannot deliver its own best analysis? The group's Swiss registration, US-hosted infrastructure and near-absent representation from post-2004 member states make that question harder, not easier, to answer in its favour.
