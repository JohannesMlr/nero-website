# New State and Governance Models — actors

People, organisations and initiatives doing notable work in this field.

## Johannes Müller — Renaissance Thoughts

NERO's own publication channel (Substack, bilingual German/English). Ongoing series on goals as an
instrument of political leadership and communication, drawing on nearly a decade of building
outcome-based operating models in large organisations. Part 1 (goal content and quality criteria) is
filed; Part 2 (goal processes and routines in parties, governments and administrations) and a companion
essay on collective identity are announced.

**Why it matters for this field:** this is where NERO's own governance position is being developed in
public. Everything filed from it is a foundation text, not independent evidence — build on it, quote it,
but never cite it as external corroboration.

Sources: `sources/2025-11-23-goals-as-political-leadership-instrument.md`

## Aladin El-Mafaalani

Sociologist, author of *Misstrauensgemeinschaften*. Argues that societies with a positive outlook on the
future can absorb considerable hardship, and that spirals of mutual distrust and radicalisation begin
only once confidence in a better tomorrow erodes.

**Why it matters for this field:** supplies the mechanism linking governance capacity to social
cohesion — the case that ambitious, credible goals are not decoration on top of good administration but
a precondition for a polity that can bear reform at all. Useful as external support for a claim NERO
otherwise makes on its own authority.

Sources: `sources/2025-11-23-goals-as-political-leadership-instrument.md`
